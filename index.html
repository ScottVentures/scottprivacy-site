FAQ

Questions people ask

Is ScottPrivacy free?

Yes. You can download and use it for free, and there are no ads.

Why does it ask to be my Phone or SMS app?

Android only lets the Phone app block calls and show a warning screen, and only the SMS app can move scam texts to Spam. You can switch back to your old apps any time in Android settings, and your messages and call history stay on your phone.

Is it safe to install from this website?

Yes, as long as you download it from this site. Android may warn you because the app isn't from Google Play; that's normal for apps installed directly. Every update is checked by the app and by Android before it installs, and you always confirm it.

Does it work on Tecno, Infinix, itel and Samsung phones?

Yes. It works on Android 7 and newer. Some phones have extra battery settings; ScottPrivacy shows you which ones to change so the call screen opens instantly.

Does it work outside Kenya?

Yes. It reads your country from the SIM card, so local numbers, foreign calls and tips are right wherever you are. Scam checks cover East Africa, India, West Africa and French- and Portuguese-speaking countries.

What does it send over the internet?

Nothing about you. Once a day it downloads a small scam-rules file and checks for updates. If you use the password check, only the first 5 characters of a scrambled code of your password are sent, never the password itself. The privacy policy lists everything.
