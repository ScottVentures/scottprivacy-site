ScottPrivacy

Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · App: ScottPrivacy (com.scottprivacy.app)

The short version

ScottPrivacy checks your calls, messages, contacts, apps and files on your phone. None of it is uploaded to us or anyone else. We don't have accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share data.

What stays on your phone

To protect you, the app reads the following. It is processed only on your device and stored only in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

Phone numbers of incoming and outgoing calls, call historyCall blocking, the call screen and your call log (only when ScottPrivacy is your Phone app, or for the caller ID & spam role)
SMS messagesShowing your messages, sorting scams into Spam, warning about suspicious messages, Code guard (only when ScottPrivacy is your SMS app)
Names in your own M-PESA / Airtel Money messagesShowing a name for numbers that aren't in your contacts. You can switch this off and erase learned names.
ContactsTelling contacts apart from unknown numbers, so contacts always get through

Wi-Fi name, network type, device settingsThe network & device audit
Your block lists, settings and check historyRemembering your choices

The only times the app uses the internet

  • Password breach check (when you use it): only the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash of the password are sent to Pwned Passwords. Your password itself never leaves the phone.
  • Email breach check (only if you add your own API key): the email address you type is sent to Have I Been Pwned to look it up.
  • Scam rule updates: about once a day the app downloads a small rules file from GitHub Pages. Nothing about you is sent; like any website, GitHub may see your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Contact support: if you tap it, your email app opens with your phone model and app version filled in. You decide whether to send it.

Google Play delivers the app and updates and may collect data under Google's privacy policy. If you buy ScottPrivacy Pro, Google Play handles the payment; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid, not your payment details.

Your control

  • Every permission is optional and can be removed in Android settings. Features that need it will stop working.
  • You can stop using ScottPrivacy as your Phone or SMS app at any time in Android settings. Your messages and call history stay on your phone.
  • Delete your data: Settings > Erase personal data, or uninstall the app. Since we hold no copy, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

ScottPrivacy is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. In fact it collects nothing at all.
