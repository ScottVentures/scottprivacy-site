The short version

ScottPrivacy checks your calls, messages, contacts, apps and files on your phone. None of it is uploaded to us or anyone else. We don't use ads, analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share data. Your free account holds only your email, name and country.

What stays on your phone

To protect you, the app reads the following. It is processed only on your device and stored only in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

The only times the app uses the internet

  • Signing in: your email and password are sent to our account service (Supabase) to sign you in, and the app checks now and then that your account is still active. See Your account.
  • Password breach check (when you use it): only the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash of the password are sent to Pwned Passwords. Your password itself never leaves the phone.
  • Email breach check (only if you add your own API key): the email address you type is sent to Have I Been Pwned to look it up.
  • Scam rule updates: about once a day the app downloads a small rules file from GitHub Pages. Nothing about you is sent; like any website, GitHub may see your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Report scam (when you tap it): only the reported number, and whether it was a call or SMS, is sent and linked to your account. Numbers reported by several people are sent to every ScottPrivacy phone about twice a day, so everyone is warned. Your messages are never sent.
  • Backup (when you're signed in): your block lists and protection settings are backed up to your account once a day, so you can restore them on a new phone.
  • Crash reports (only if you agree): if the app closes unexpectedly, it asks before sending technical details: phone model, Android version and the error. No messages, numbers, contacts or files.
  • App updates (copies downloaded from this website): about once a day the app downloads a small file, update/version.json, to see if there is a newer version. If you choose to update, the new app file is downloaded from GitHub, checked, and Android asks you to confirm. Nothing about you is sent. Copies from Google Play or Palm Store are updated by that store instead.
  • Contact support: if you tap it, your email app opens with your phone model and app version filled in. You decide whether to send it.

Google Play delivers the app and updates and may collect data under Google's privacy policy. If you buy ScottPrivacy Pro, Google Play handles the payment; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid, not your payment details.

Your control

  • Every permission is optional and can be removed in Android settings. Features that need it will stop working.
  • You can stop using ScottPrivacy as your Phone or SMS app at any time in Android settings. Your messages and call history stay on your phone.
  • Delete your data: Settings > Erase personal data, or uninstall the app. To delete your account together with your scam reports, backup and review on our side, use Delete my account on your account page.

Children

ScottPrivacy is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

This website

The website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other companies. If you sign in, your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in; it is used for nothing else. The help assistant runs in your browser, so your questions aren't sent anywhere.

If you send a message with the contact form, it is delivered to us by Web3Forms and we receive your name, email, phone model, rating and message. We use them only to reply and to improve ScottPrivacy, and delete them when they're no longer needed. Download numbers come from GitHub's public release counts, which contain nothing about who downloaded.

Your account

You need a free account to download the app and to rate it, and the app asks you to sign in with the same account. Signing in only sends your email and password (to check them) and keeps you signed in; what the app checks on your phone (calls, messages, files) is still never uploaded. For your account we store, at Supabase: your email address, your password (only in scrambled form), the name and country you give us, when you joined and last signed in, and which versions of the app you downloaded from this website and when. We use this only to run your account, keep downloads and ratings honest, and see how many people use ScottPrivacy. We never sell it or show your email to anyone.

You can change your name, country and password on your account page, and delete the whole account there at any time. Deleting it removes your profile, your review and your download history straight away.

Ratings and reviews

Ratings are tied to your account, so each person rates once (you can change it later). Every rating counts in the score shown on the site. Written reviews are shown publicly with your first name and country only after we check them, and never with your email. To remove your review, delete it from your account page or use the contact form.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR or any other privacy law: use the contact form.
