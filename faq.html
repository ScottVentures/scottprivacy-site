FAQ

Questions people ask

Is ScottPrivacy free?

Yes. You can download and use it for free, and there are no ads.

Why does it ask to be my Phone or SMS app?

Android only lets the Phone app block calls and show a warning screen, and only the SMS app can move scam texts to Spam. You can switch back to your old apps any time in Android settings, and your messages and call history stay on your phone.

Is it safe to install from this website?

Yes, as long as you download it from this site. Android may show a warning because the app isn't from Google Play; tap More details › Install anyway. Every update is checked by the app and by Android before it installs, and you always confirm it.

Play Protect blocked or warned about the app

Some phones show a Play Protect warning for apps installed from a website. Tap More details › Install anyway. If it says the app was blocked, open the Play Store, tap your profile picture › Play Protect › the settings icon, turn off Scan apps with Play Protect for a moment, install ScottPrivacy, then turn it back on.

Does it work on Tecno, Infinix, itel and Samsung phones?

Yes. It works on Android 7 and newer. Some phones have extra battery settings; ScottPrivacy shows you which ones to change so the call screen opens instantly.

Does it work outside Kenya?

Yes. It reads your country from the SIM card, so local numbers, foreign calls and tips are right wherever you are. Scam checks cover East Africa, India, West Africa and French- and Portuguese-speaking countries.

What does it send over the internet?

Your calls, messages and files never leave your phone. The app signs you in, sends numbers you choose to report as scams, backs up your block lists once a day, and sends a crash report only if you agree. It also downloads scam rules and updates. The privacy policy lists everything.
